About

Tokoyo Musubi Shrine is a place of prayer for people today.

Prayers also turn toward new places born of human activity.

As a place that connects hearts without being bound by location, it carries on the practice of shrine visitation.

Coming when you wish, turning your heart toward it, then returning to daily life.

In that moment, Tokoyo Musubi Shrine.
