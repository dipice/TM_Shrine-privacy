6. AI-Generated Content

6.1 Readings and Conversations with the Girl

The App uses Google's generative AI service, Gemini, for omikuji readings and conversations with the girl on the approach to the shrine. A reading generates text based on the words of the omikuji and your prayer or wish. A conversation with the girl generates text based on the omikuji at that time and the flow of the conversation.

6.2 Disclaimer

  • Users must comply with all applicable laws and regulations

6.4 Data Handling

To generate readings and responses, the App sends the information needed for that purpose to Google's Gemini service. This may include prayers and wishes, the words of omikuji, messages to the girl, and the preceding conversation.

The App stores readings and conversations with the girl on its servers to retry after an interrupted connection, resume a conversation, and prevent duplicate use of Omikuji Tokens or Go-enn Coins. Please see the Privacy Policy for details about the information stored and when it is deleted.

7. Subscription Service (Monthly Musubi / 月結び)

7.1 Service Overview

TokoyoMusubi Shrine offers an optional monthly subscription service called "Monthly Musubi" (月結び).

  • Price: US$1.99/month in the United States (auto-renewing). Prices vary by country or region. Please check the price displayed by your store before purchasing.
  • Monthly Musubi 20 (月結び20): 20 Go-enn Coins granted upon initial purchase and each monthly renewal

  • Go-enn Coin validity: 2 months from grant date (subscription-granted coins only)
  • Maximum balance: 200 Go-enn Coins (subscription-granted coins only). Each grant is limited to the remaining capacity.

7.2 Go-enn Coin (五縁玉)

Go-enn Coins can be offered during shrine visits to receive Omikuji Tokens. A standard omikuji draw uses an Omikuji Token first; if none is available, one Go-enn Coin may be used instead.

  • Go-enn Coin cannot be exchanged for cash or refunded
  • Go-enn Coin cannot be transferred to other users
  • Consumption order: Coins closest to expiration are consumed first
  • Expired Go-enn Coin will be automatically removed
