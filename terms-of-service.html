7. Subscription Service (Monthly Musubi / 月結び)

7.1 Service Overview

Tokoyo Musubi Shrine offers an optional monthly subscription service called "Monthly Musubi" (月結び).

  • Price: Auto-renewing monthly. Prices vary by country or region. Please check the price displayed by your store before purchasing.
  • Monthly Musubi 20 (月結び20): 20 Go-enn Coins granted upon initial purchase and each monthly renewal
  • Go-enn Coin validity: 2 months from grant date (subscription-granted coins only)
  • Negikoto and the Sanpai Notebook: Everyone can write one "Today’s Negikoto" and view past entries in the notebook. Monthly Musubi members can write longer Negikoto.
  • Member-only notebook features: Monthly Musubi members can copy an individual past entry into "Today’s Negikoto" and copy all text from the notebook’s past entries at once.

7.2 Go-enn Coin (五縁玉)

Go-enn Coins can be offered during shrine visits to receive Omikuji Tokens. A standard omikuji draw uses an Omikuji Token first; if none is available, one Go-enn Coin may be used instead.

  • Go-enn Coin cannot be exchanged for cash or refunded
  • Go-enn Coin cannot be transferred to other users
  • Consumption order: Coins closest to expiration are consumed first
  • Expired Go-enn Coin will be automatically removed

7.3 Cancellation

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time through App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) settings
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
  • Subscription-granted Go-enn Coin can still be used until their expiration date

7.4 Refunds

Please request refunds in accordance with the refund policies of the App Store or Google Play. If a refund changes your subscription status, access to membership benefits may end.

7.5 Data Storage and Restoration

  • Subscription status: Managed by RevenueCat and can be restored after reinstalling the app using the "Restore membership benefits" feature within the app
  • Go-enn Coin balance: Stored on our servers and linked to your anonymous app account. You may be unable to carry over your Go-enn Coins after reinstalling the app, changing devices, or similar changes. Restoring your subscription does not guarantee restoration of your coin balance.
  • Negikoto and the Sanpai Notebook:

    Negikoto, past entries, stamp positions, and other notebook information are stored on your device and do not sync to other devices. After your membership ends, you can still view the content remaining on your device and use your existing Negikoto.

    The notebook stores up to 100 past entries. When this limit is exceeded, the oldest entries are deleted first. "Restore membership benefits" does not restore notebook contents. Restoration of notebook contents after deleting or reinstalling the app or changing devices is not guaranteed.

  • After account deletion: Deleted data cannot be restored. For details, please see Account and Data Deletion.

8. Offering Purses (奉納賽銭)

Offering purses are consumable in-app purchases. Each purchase provides one purse for a single offering experience, in which the purse is offered at the Haiden (worship hall) in the App. A Monthly Musubi subscription is not required.

  • The Go-enn Coins inside a purse are used only for offering that purse. They are not added to the shared Go-enn Coin balance and cannot be used for other purposes, such as omikuji.
  • Purchased purses do not expire. A purse remains available until it is offered at the Haiden. While you hold an unused purse, the four offering purse products cannot be purchased again.
  • After the visit, Omikuji Tokens are granted depending on the product. Combined with tokens granted for regular coin offerings, up to 5 Omikuji Tokens can be granted per shrine visit. Tokens beyond the holding limit of 999 cannot be received. Please check the App for the validity period of Omikuji Tokens.
  • Purses that have not been offered at the Haiden cannot be carried over after changing devices or deleting your account. "Restore membership benefits" does not restore purses.
  • Prices are shown on the purchase screen. Please request refunds in accordance with the refund policies of the App Store or Google Play.
