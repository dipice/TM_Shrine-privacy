Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TokoyoMusubi Shrine handles information when you use this mobile application.

1. Information I Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

You do not need to register your name or contact details. The app uses anonymous authentication (Firebase Authentication) and stores the following information linked to your anonymous user ID:

  • Progress, balances, and records of purchases, worship, and omikuji
  • Readings, conversations with the girl, and reports
  • Records such as conversation counts and topic usage, to continue conversations and avoid repetition

App preferences and settings are stored locally on your device.

Negikoto (your own prayer words) are stored on your device. When a reading is generated, the Negikoto in use is sent for that purpose, and its content may appear in the generated reading.

2. Face Data

What Face Data Is Collected

This app uses on-device face detection to detect:

  • Face position and head tilt (for bow detection)
  • Eye open/closed state (for prayer detection)

This data is processed entirely on your device in real-time.

How Face Data Is Used

Face data is used solely to detect gestures during worship (bowing and prayer). It is not used for identification or any other purpose.

Data Storage

Face data is processed in memory only and is not stored on your device or any server.

Data Retention

Face data is discarded immediately after each frame is processed. No face data is retained.

Third-Party Sharing

Face data is not shared with any third parties. No images or facial feature data are transmitted to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Face Detection Technologies

This app uses the following technologies for on-device face detection. All processing occurs locally on your device.

Apple ARKit (iOS)

On iOS devices with TrueDepth camera (Face ID-enabled devices), the app uses Apple ARKit for high-precision face tracking. ARKit processes facial data entirely on your device.

  • ARKit Documentation
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Google ML Kit (iOS fallback / Android)

On iOS devices without TrueDepth camera and on all Android devices, the app uses Google ML Kit for face detection.

  • Google ML Kit Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

3.2 Apple App Store and Google Play

App distribution and purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, as applicable. TokoyoMusubi Shrine does not store payment information.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

3.3 Firebase Services

This app uses Firebase (provided by Google) for the following purposes:

  • Firebase Authentication: Anonymous sign-in to sync your progress across sessions
  • Cloud Firestore: Storing the information described in Section 1.1 in the cloud
  • Firebase Remote Config: Delivering app configuration updates without requiring an app update
  • Firebase Analytics: Understanding app usage patterns to improve the experience (opt-in only)
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collecting crash reports to improve app stability (opt-in only)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Managing notification tokens and settings and delivering push notifications about updates and announcements

Firebase is used for anonymous authentication, storing the information described above, providing app features, deleting information after its retention period, and managing notification tokens and settings. Data is stored on Google's servers. Even with an anonymous ID, information you enter or purchase information may relate to an individual. For details, please refer to:

  • Firebase Privacy Policy

3.4 RevenueCat

This app uses RevenueCat for subscription and in-app purchase management.

  • Purchase Processing: RevenueCat validates purchase receipts and manages subscription status
  • User ID: Purchase information is linked to your user ID. Even with an anonymous ID, purchase information may relate to an individual.
  • Subscription Restoration: Purchase history is stored on RevenueCat servers, enabling subscription restoration across reinstalls via the "Restore Purchases" feature

Note: Go-enn Coin balances are stored on Firebase servers and linked to your anonymous app account. You may be unable to carry over your Go-enn Coins after reinstalling the app, changing devices, or similar changes. Restoring your subscription through RevenueCat does not guarantee restoration of your coin balance.

For details, please refer to:

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

3.5 AI Services

The app uses Google Gemini for readings and the OpenAI API for conversations with the girl, including conversations after drawing an omikuji. Messages are used to generate and adjust responses to fit the flow of the conversation.

Gemini receives the words of the wish and omikuji. OpenAI receives the current and immediately preceding messages, the girl's responses, and other information about the flow of the conversation. Depending on the conversation, the words of the wish and omikuji and the reading results are also sent. Please do not enter information that could identify a person, such as a name, address, or contact details. If you enter it, that information may also be sent.

Each provider may retain information under its own terms and privacy policies. This retention is separate from deletion by the app.

  • Gemini API Additional Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI API Data Controls

4. Your Privacy Choices

Improvement Support

You can change your participation in usage analytics and crash diagnostics through the "Improvement Support" option in Settings. Information needed to provide features such as authentication, purchases, notifications, and AI generation is handled separately from this setting.

Camera Permission

You may control camera access through your device settings. If camera access is disabled, gesture-based features will be unavailable.

Photo Library Permission

When you save a Kotonoha Fortune result as an image, the app asks for permission to add photos to your photo library. The app only adds the image you choose to save; it does not read your photos. The image is created on your device and is not uploaded. You may control this permission through your device settings.

5. Data Retention

Local app data is stored on your device and will be removed when you uninstall the app.

The text of readings and conversations is deleted by a scheduled process after 24 hours have elapsed from the start of each respective processing operation. This includes the words of the wish and omikuji and the reading results. Adding messages does not extend the retention period. Deletion may be delayed depending on processing conditions.

Text and additional details submitted in a report are retained for 90 days from submission for review and improvement, then deleted by a scheduled process. A copy included in a report is retained for this period even after the regular reading or conversation text is deleted.

Conversation records other than the text are retained separately, linked to your anonymous account, to continue conversations and avoid repetition. They are not all deleted as part of the 24-hour text deletion. Other data, such as balances and worship records, is also retained in association with your anonymous account.

For inquiries about deletion of cloud data, please contact us via email. Backups and similar copies follow each service's retention and recovery procedures; immediate deletion of all copies is not guaranteed.

AI providers may retain information separately, as described in Section 3.5.

Face data is not retained; it is discarded immediately after processing each camera frame.

6. International Data Handling

Third-party services may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States, in accordance with their privacy policies.
